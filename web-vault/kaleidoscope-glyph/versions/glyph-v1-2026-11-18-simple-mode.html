  • Forget the passkey and the glyph cannot be opened. There is no reset.
  • The locked data lives in the PNG file's data, not in its pixels. If a website or app compresses, re-saves or shrinks the image, the data is lost. Send glyphs as file attachments and keep a backup.
  • The title is locked inside the glyph. The picture only shows the format, the save time and a short ID.
  • Locking and unlocking take a few seconds on purpose. That slowness protects your passkey.
