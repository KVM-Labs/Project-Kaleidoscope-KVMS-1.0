TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN OF THE MANIFEST ARCHITECTURE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "permissions": 
   * "activeTab": Allows the extension to safely read the text inside your current AI chat tab when you click "Save."
   * "storage": Saves your local settings and preferences directly into the browser's encrypted local storage.
   * "downloads": Grants the extension permission to save the final 50KB Kaleidoscope PNG directly to your computer's local Downloads folder.

2. "content_scripts" -> "matches": 
   Specifies exactly which ecosystems the engine interacts with natively. Restricts the background script from reading unauthorized tabs (like financial or social platforms) to preserve baseline structural user privacy.

3. "background" -> "service_worker": 
   Points to `worker.js`, the silent local background engine. Manages the cryptographic math and converts raw text arrays into visual geometry without passing data across cloud servers.
